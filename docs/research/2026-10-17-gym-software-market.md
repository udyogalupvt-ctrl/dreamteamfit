# Gym software market research: what makes the top products top

Date: 2026-10-17. Purpose: decide what our AI gym software must have to be the best, and where it can win.

How this was made: web research by three agents (global leaders, the Indian market, AI and migration). Most prices and claims come from vendors' own pages or competitors' blogs, and G2/Capterra blocked direct reading, so treat every number as "reported", not proven. Open the source page before quoting any number publicly. "Not confirmed" means it could not be checked.

---

## 1. The short answer

1. **Nobody sells a real AI CFO.** Competitors' AI is a virtual front desk (answers calls/chats) or a "members at risk" list. No gym product found forecasts the owner's cash, renewals and shortfalls in advance. This is our open lane.
2. **What owners hate most** (global and India, same order): price rises and hidden fees, slow or no support, contracts that are hard to leave, bugs after updates.
3. **What the best-loved products share** (PushPress, Gymdesk, TeamUp): clear price, no contract, free and fast migration, quick human support.
4. **India is a value market.** About 46,500 gyms, 80% of them value gyms with a membership fee of under ₹8,000–14,000 a year. Most small gyms run on paper, Excel and personal WhatsApp.
5. **No Indian product gives fingerprint + official WhatsApp under about ₹10,000 a year**, and almost none speaks Telugu or other regional languages. Only one (Fitplex) claims migration from paper registers.

## 3. The Indian market

**Market:** about 46,500 commercial gyms, 12.3M members, about ₹16,200 cr revenue (Deloitte/HFA 2025). Fragmented; few national chains (Cult.fit about 708 centres, Anytime Fitness 150+). Gym GST moved to 5% (no input credit) from 22 Sep 2025.

**Price bands (per year):**

| Band | Range | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Cheap | ₹2,500–10,000 | GymBook ~₹1,800–2,700, GoGym4U ₹2,499, GymShakti ₹3,299–9,999, DGymbook ~₹6,000, GymPilot ₹3,600–8,400 |
| Mid | ₹10,000–45,000 | Torzil ₹14,999, MyGymDesk ₹9,999–44,999, FitGymSoftware ₹11,999, gymkhata.com ₹15,600–30,000 |
| Premium | ₹45,000+ | Fitness Manager up to ₹75,000, GymMaster/Wellyx ~₹1–3.8 lakh, Mindbody/Glofox |

Most Indian vendors hold back WhatsApp, fingerprint and the member app for the middle tiers. Pay-per-member pricing is common (₹6–20 per active member).

**What Indian owners need (must-haves):** renewal and dues reminders that actually work, part payments, fingerprint/face machines (ZKTeco/eSSL) that lock expired members, GST invoices (5% now), UPI QR / payment links / UPI AutoPay, the official WhatsApp API (owners fear bans on unofficial ones), staff limits and an action log (cash leakage), PT fees and trainer commissions kept separate, free migration, and support that answers.

**Play Store complaints of the popular Indian apps:** bugs and slow apps after updates, OTP login failures, duplicated or lost members, fee changes not saving, silent price rises, support that never replies.

**Gaps no Indian product fills well:**
1. Reliable support and stable updates.
2. Clear, metered AI and WhatsApp pricing (nobody publishes this).
3. Telugu / regional languages in the owner and member apps.
4. Migration from paper registers.
5. Anti-leakage controls built for staff cash theft.
6. Fingerprint + official WhatsApp at the cheap price.

**What churn prediction uses:** visits per week, days since last visit, how long they've been a member, spend, no-shows, failed payments. A Gymdesk benchmark (801k memberships): 24-month cancellation is 44% for rare visitors vs 23% for 3+ visits a week. Global average yearly member retention is 66.4% (HFA 2025), so about 1 in 3 members leaves each year.

**Finance tools outside gyms (what a "world best" CFO is measured against):** Float, Fathom and Jirav do rolling 13-week and 12-month cash forecasts with what-if scenarios; QuickBooks plans 24 months; RazorpayX (India, beta) shows how long cash will last and chases unpaid invoices. None of these knows a gym's renewals, attendance or trainer payouts. Our CFO can.

## 5. Migration

**What competitors do:** free migration is standard (PushPress, Fitplex, GymShakti, MyGymDesk, Torzil, Easy Gym), usually done by their team in 1 day to 3 weeks from Excel or CSV. Some charge ($500–5,000 GymMaster; fees at Wodify). Members and renewal dates usually move; payment history and schedules often don't.

**AI import tools** (Dromo, OneSchema, Flatfile) match columns automatically and check rows, but cost $599+/month. For a few hundred rows per gym, one cheap AI call plus our own checking code is enough.

**Reading handwritten registers with AI:** the best models get roughly 85–95% of fields right on neat English tables; phone numbers and amounts are the riskiest. Telugu handwriting is weaker. **A staff check screen is mandatory.** No gym product does photo-to-rows today.

**Recommended approach:**
1. Download template OR upload any export. AI sees the headings + 5 sample rows and proposes the column matching; the owner confirms.
2. Our code does the cleaning: phone format, day-first vs month-first dates, plan names, duplicates. Preview with problem rows highlighted.
3. Register photos: one page per AI call → rows with a confidence per field → side-by-side photo/rows screen, low-confidence fields highlighted, phone (10 digits) and page totals checked against the written totals.
4. Everything lands in a holding area first, never straight into live data. Original files kept. Undo per run.
5. Pilot on 2–3 real registers before promising it in sales.

**Privacy (India DPDP Act):** rules notified 14 Nov 2025; most duties start about 14 May 2027. The gym owns the data, we process it. For migration AI: use a paid API tier (free tiers may train on data), send only what's needed, delete photos after import, add a consent line to the gym contract, get a legal opinion before 2027. The CFO keeps its rule: only totals, never names or phones.

## 8. Our lane (proposal for discussion)

**Positioning:** "The gym software that tells you the future": AI CFO + the easiest switch in India + honest yearly pricing.

**What should make us number one:**
1. **AI CFO**: cash forecast, renewal pipeline, "short by ₹X on date Y" warnings, at-risk members with the reason and the next step, plain-language questions with the formula shown. Nobody does this.
2. **Easiest migration**: free, template or any export with AI matching, register photos read by AI with a check screen. Nobody does photos.
3. **Leak-proof money**: Day Book, every edit logged, owner alerts. Indian owners' top fear.
4. **Fingerprint + official WhatsApp even on the cheapest plan**: no Indian product does this under ₹10,000.
5. **Telugu (then Hindi and others)** for staff and members.
6. **Honest pricing, no surprises**: yearly price fixed for the year, AI and WhatsApp shown as metered usage, no lock-in, data export any time. This attacks the number one complaint worldwide.
7. **Support that answers** + stable updates (staged releases, tests): the top complaint on Indian Play Store apps.

**Tier shape to discuss** (numbers are placeholders until the user decides the top price): a member-count ladder like Gymdesk combined with a feature ladder like PushPress. Lowest plan ₹6,000/year with no AI; AI CFO from the second plan; multi-branch and chain tools at the top. AI and WhatsApp billed as usage on every plan that has them.
